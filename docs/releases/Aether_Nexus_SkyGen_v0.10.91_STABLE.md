# Aether Nexus SkyGen v0.10.91 Stable

The project owner confirmed that v0.10.91 cleared gameplay testing. The stable installer is the exact archive used in testing. Bedrock displays the original “Player Head Trophy Fix Test” pack names on import.

[Bedrock add-on](../../releases/Aether_Nexus_SkyGen_v0.10.91_STABLE.mcaddon) · [Matching source ZIP](../../releases/Aether_Nexus_SkyGen_v0.10.91_Source.zip)

## Player head trophies

Newly placed player head trophies save their location. Trophy displays resist knockback, stop drifting, and cannot damage players. Pick up and re-place an older trophy to save a new anchor if it was already displaced.

## Verification

The trophy regression checks on Bedrock Dedicated Server 1.26.63.1 covered a PvP death producing a named trophy, placement and anchoring under simulated forces, trophy-attributed damage protection, crouch recovery, pickup permissions, and persistence after restart.
